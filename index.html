Scientific Challenge

Moving Beyond Conventional Detection Paradigms

METHYLOX™ explores computational and molecular approaches that aim to identify cancer-associated epigenetic signals before structural abnormalities become clinically observable.

Current Detection Limitations

Conventional screening methods primarily rely on imaging or tissue abnormalities that become detectable after biological changes have progressed. Earlier molecular alterations may remain difficult to observe using morphology-based approaches alone.

Computational Epigenetic Framework

METHYLOX™ combines CRISPR-Cas12 molecular sensing, DNA methylation biomarkers candidates, fragmentomics and artificial intelligence within a unified pre-clinical research platform "designed to discover and evaluate cancer-associated molecular signals, including ultra-low abundance circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) features, using computational analysis and public genomic datasets."
