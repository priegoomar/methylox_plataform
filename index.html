Clinical Paradigm

Overcoming Screening Barriers

Analyzing the gap between tissue-dependent modalities and mathematical ultra-early signal resolution.

Late-Stage Diagnostic Overreliance

Current diagnostic standards depend on structural tissue anomalies detectable via medical imaging or physical palpation. This creates an inherent timeline latency, as cell abnormalities multiply significantly before presenting visible morphological structures, reducing alternative strategic windows.

Epigenetic & Enzymatic Targeting

METHYLOX™ implements an integrated system combining CRISPR-Cas12 biochemical specificity with epigenetic biomarker validation. By isolation of ultra-low volume circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) fragments within a computational evaluation matrix, molecular variations are contextualized before structural physical development occurs.

Pre-clinical Scientific Foundation

The evaluation of early oncological alerts rests upon robust, non-promotional genomic mechanisms. Our core research tracks clear cellular and bioinformatic events to extract measurable value without clinical diagnostic assumptions.

Focus vectors include:

  • • Epigenetics: Monitoring differential target hypermethylation variables as primary signals.
  • • ctDNA: Characterizing cell-free circulating fragment properties in liquid biopsy substrates.
  • • CRISPR Systems: Refining target Cas12 tracking systems to minimize physical sample requirements.
  • • Bioinformatics: Advancing baseline sorting logic through rigorous open dataset testing environments.
