Research Platform

Computational Design for High-Sensitivity Molecular Detection

METHYLOX™ combines computational biomarker discovery, liquid biopsy research and CRISPR-based molecular sensing to investigate subtle molecular signals associated with early breast cancer.

  • Selection of high-priority epigenetic biomarkers from public genomic datasets.
  • Computationally designed CRISPR guides optimized to investigate low-abundance molecular biomarkers.
  • Integrated software that analyzes candidate biomarkers and supports research interpretation.
"Designed to support the investigation of molecular signals that may be present at very low concentrations during the earliest stages of disease." — METHYLOX™ Research Platform
