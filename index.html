Scientific Challenge

Advancing Early Breast Cancer Detection

Breast cancer begins with molecular alterations that may appear before clinical signs become detectable. METHYLOX™ is being developed to investigate these early biological signals through a computational pre-clinical platform.

Current Approaches

Most screening methods detect breast cancer after structural changes have already developed.

Our Approach

METHYLOX™ investigates DNA methylation biomarkers, CRISPR-Cas12 and fragmentomics to support research in early breast cancer detection.
